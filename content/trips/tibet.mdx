Since there is little to no infrastructure in Tibet, even a simple road journey may be exciting and unpredictable. Tibet is carefully opening up to the western world and providing visitors with a gratifying real experience. (At least we think so).

Local monasteries are attempting to find a position in a nation that is crashing into the twentieth century after centuries of being practically isolated. Tibet is the ideal place to experience unique cuisine while traveling spiritually and feel internally renewed while traveling back in time. For those who appreciate culture, there are exhibits of people who are upbeat and proud of their religion and beliefs, much as they were 100 years ago.

The vast majority of this territory is located above **4000 meters (13,000 feet)**, where it holds many secrets. This creates the perfect environment for exploring the highest Himalayas in the world, providing you with the most exciting opportunities for **trekking, climbing, and expedition; cultural tours and sightseeing; and cycling and mountain biking.**

## Popular Destinations

- Mount Kailash
- Everest Base Camp (North Side)
- Lhasa & Potala Palace
