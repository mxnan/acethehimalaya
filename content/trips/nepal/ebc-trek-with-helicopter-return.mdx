#### Highlights of the Trip

- Fly to **Lukla**, the thrilling gateway to the Khumbu region.
- Trek through Sherpa villages, crossing suspension bridges over the **Dudh Koshi River** on the way to **Namche Bazaar**.
- Discover Namche Bazaar, the vibrant **Sherpa hub** where tradition, culture, and stunning Himalayan views come together.
- Explore **Sagarmatha National Park**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site rich in Himalayan flora, fauna, and mountain scenery.
- Visit the historic **Tengboche Monastery**, framed by **Ama Dablam** and panoramic Himalayan peaks.
- Hike to **Nangkartsang Peak (5,083 m/16,676 ft)** during your acclimatization in **Dingboche**.
- Get a glimpse of the **Khumbu Glacier** and **Khumbu Icefall** en route to **Everest Base Camp (5,364 m/17,598 ft)**.
- Stand beneath Mount **Everest (8,848.86 m)** and other Himalayan giants.
- Hike to **Kala Patthar (5,555 m/18,225 ft)** for a spectacular sunrise over Mount Everest, Lhotse, and Nuptse.
- Enjoy a scenic **helicopter flight** from Gorak Shep to Lukla, soaking in unforgettable aerial views of the Everest region.
