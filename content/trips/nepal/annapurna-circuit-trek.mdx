#### Highlights of the Trip

- Drive along the scenic mountain road from Kathmandu to Dharapani.
- Ascend through the captivating **Marshyangdi River** valley.
- Meet and experience the rich cultures of the Gurung, Magar, and Thakali communities, known for their warm hospitality.
- Witness towering Himalayan peaks including **Annapurna I, II, III, IV, Gangapurna, Dhaulagiri, Manaslu, and Machhapuchhare**.
- Walk through lush rhododendron forests, scenic alpine meadows, and remote mountain villages.
- Cross the challenging **Thorong La Pass (5,416 m / 17,769 ft)**, one of the highest trekking passes in the world.
- Visit the sacred pilgrimage site of **Muktinath**, revered by both Hindus and Buddhists.
- Explore Jomsom, Marpha, and Tukuche, charming villages famous for their apple orchards and locally made apple brandy.
- Drive through the world’s deepest gorge in the Kali Gandaki valley.
- Relax with a soothing bath in the natural hot springs of **Tatopani**.
- Enjoy breathtaking sunrise views from **Poon Hill**, with panoramic vistas of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges.
