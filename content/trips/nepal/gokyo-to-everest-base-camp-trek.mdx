#### Highlights of the Trip

- Captivating aerial views of the Himalayas on the scenic flight to Lukla Airport.
- Majestic trek to the pristine **Gokyo Lakes**, one of the world’s highest freshwater lakes.
- Immerse yourself in the lively culture and traditions of the Sherpa community.
- Cross the **Cho La Pass**, one of the three major high mountain passes in the Everest region.
- Witness panoramic Himalayan views from **Gokyo Ri**, including Mount Everest, Cho Oyu, and Makalu.
- Hike to **Kala Patthar (5,555 m/18,225 ft)**, the highest point of the trek.
- Trek to **Everest Base Camp (5,364 m/17,598 ft)**, the base of the world’s highest mountain.
- Visit Tengboche Monastery, the largest and most significant monastery in the Khumbu region
