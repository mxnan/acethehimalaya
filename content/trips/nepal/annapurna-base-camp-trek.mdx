#### Highlights of the Trip

- Discover the beautiful lakeside city of **Pokhara**, the gateway to the Annapurna region.
- Witness an impressive sunrise from Poon Hill, illuminating the snow-capped Himalayan peaks.
- Enjoy breathtaking views of the world’s highest mountains, including **Dhaulagiri I (7th highest), Annapurna I (10th highest), Machapuchare (Fishtail), and Hiunchuli.**
- Explore **Machapuchare Base Camp** before entering the heart of the Annapurna Sanctuary.
- Spend the night at Annapurna Base Camp, surrounded by towering peaks.
- Trek through wilderness rich in diverse flora and fauna.
- Capture the spellbinding vistas of mountain and glacier from Annapurna Base Camp.
- Experience the warm hospitality and traditions of the Gurung and Magar communities.
- Relax in a natural hot spring bath at Jhinu Danda after your ABC trek.
