#### Trip Highlights

- Explore Kathmandu’s spiritual sites, including Pashupatinath and Boudhanath Temples.
- Visit Lhasa’s iconic Potala Palace, a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Experience the spiritual significance of Jokhang Temple in Lhasa.
- Stroll through Lhasa’s vibrant Barkhor Market.
- Enjoy breathtaking Himalayan views during the flight between Kathmandu and Lhasa.
