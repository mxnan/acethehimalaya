Boasting eight of the fourteen 8000-ers including the likes of Mt. Everest and Mt. Kanchenjunga, Nepal is synonymous to the Himalayas. The image of the glistening Himalayas is etched in the mind of every traveler who dreams of Nepal.

And for the longest time, the only way to experience the mountain majesty up close was by embarking on long multi day treks like the Everest Base Camp Trek, which used to be a popular choice.

However, with the shifting dynamics of tourism in the country, what if we told you that the heart stopping grandeur of the Himalayas is not reserved solely for the seasoned trekker?

Yes, the magic of mountains has been accessible to all. All thanks to the viewpoints in Nepal which gives you the perfect views of these snowcapped giants.

Therefore, in this blog, we delve into some easy and accessible Himalayan viewpoints in Nepal, proving that you don’t need to conquer a trail to be conquered by the view. Keep your seatbelts on!
