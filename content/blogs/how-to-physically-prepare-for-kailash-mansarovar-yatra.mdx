Located in the Trans Himalayan region of Tibet Autonomous region, **Mount Kailash** is a stunning spectacle as well as a peak that has high religious sentiments and beliefs attached to it.

Therefore, every year thousands of pilgrims from different religions and from all over the world embark on the **Kailash Mansarovar Yatra** which is regarded as one of the greatest pilgrimages of all times.

This journey turns out to be a once in a lifetime experience for most of the visitors as you will be treated to stunning views, pinch of adventures, and lots of spiritual value and **pilgrimage activities**.

However, this journey is not like any other spiritual detour, rather this is one filled with physical challenges. Given the climatic conditions and the high-altitude region which you need to explore, consider this one of the more challenging treks.
