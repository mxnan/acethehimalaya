Embarking on the iconic **Annapurna Circuit Trek** means stepping into one of Nepal’s most diverse long-distance trekking routes, where landscapes, culture, and climate change dramatically with altitude and season.

While the trail remains open year-round, not every month for Annapurna circuit trek are best for the same comfort, visibility, or safety. Choosing the right season can make a difference in weather conditions, trail accessibility, mountain views, and overall trekking experience, especially when crossing high passes like Thorong La.

The best time for Annapurna Circuit Trek is during the **Spring (March to May)** and **Autumn (September to November)** seasons. These months offer stable weather, clear skies, comfortable temperatures, and excellent visibility of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges.
