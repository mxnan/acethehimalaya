Ace the Himalaya is an idea, that aims to share with the world the passion of travel in the Himalayan region and assist visitors in achieving their desire to create everlasting memories.

At Ace the Himalaya, our mission is to craft unforgettable lifetime journeys across Nepal, Bhutan, and Tibet while prioritizing sustainability, ethical practices, and exceptional customer satisfaction.

As a Travelife Certified, we are dedicated to protecting the natural and cultural heritage of the regions we explore, reducing environmental impact through eco-friendly initiatives like water filters, waste collection, tree plantation, and promoting responsible tourism at every step.

Mission Statement:

- At Ace the Himalaya, our mission is to craft exceptional and safe adventure experiences that honor and preserve the natural environment, uplift local communities, and promote sustainable tourism practices.
- Our mission is to create unforgettable experiences for our clients while promoting sustainable tourism. We are dedicated to preserving Nepal’s breathtaking natural environment, rich cultures, timeless traditions, and treasured heritage.
- Through socially responsible, eco-friendly, and ethical treks, we aim to inspire meaningful connections and sustainable positive impacts.
- We are dedicated to minimizing our ecological footprint by implementing responsible waste management, reducing carbon emissions, and adhering to the principles of ‘leave no trace’ during our treks such as using Katadyn water filters, and collecting waste in garbage bags.
- We collaborate closely with our partners, suppliers, stakeholders, and guests to foster sustainability awareness and encourage the adoption of eco-friendly practices.
- Through our corporate social responsibility initiative, Sambhav Nepal, we actively engage in community development projects, including rebuilding homes, hostels, and schools, to enhance the well-being of the regions and local communities we explore.
- Our commitment to transparency, compliance with national regulations, and continuous improvement in sustainability standards ensures that our operations contribute positively to the environment, society, and cultural heritage- an aesthetics of Nepal.
